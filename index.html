Hardware & System Architecture

How our electronic machinery delivers industrial-grade precision.

Closed-Loop Servo Motion Control

Our machines utilize optical linear encoders paired with direct-drive linear motors. This completely eliminates mechanical backlash, allowing exact positional accuracy down to sub-micron scales during high-speed placement and milling.

  • Real-time error correction up to 10,000 times per second
  • Thermal compensation sensors to offset metal expansion
  • Zero maintenance magnetic drive shafts
